Our story

Built from neighborly rituals and good ingredients.

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: share warm bread and thoughtful hospitality with the people living and working around us. What started as a neighborhood bake table grew into a bakery known for dependable loaves, comforting pastries, and a friendly counter that feels like home.

Sourcing philosophy

Simple ingredients, mindful sourcing.

We prioritize local mills, growers, and small-batch producers whenever possible. We use organic ingredients where it fits our budget and work closely with suppliers who value quality and transparency.
